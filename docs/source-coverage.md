## Known gaps

The India portal root could not be retrieved during research; its official search-confirmed reporting pages are labeled separately. Search-confirmed resources are distinct from retrieved pages. No source has completed specialist review or a provider-account workflow test.

Bank-specific disputes, carriers, routers, gaming accounts, email variants, workplace systems, region-specific identity remedies, precise source timestamps, published/update dates, canonical metadata and permitted-use records need more research. General routes and links are offered where detailed verified variants are absent. All cybersecurity scenarios are not covered.

Manual HEAD availability checks are protected and logged. Broad extraction, content diffing, review/version rollback, and automatic rechecks are not connected. Original summaries avoid bulk copying forum stories or copyrighted guides.
